The LIGHT and DARK icons must have exactly the same geometry. Concept A: FOUR OPENING PETALS. A minimal four-part aperture / blooming launch gesture, four solid softly rounded chamfered wedge petals, arranged radially around a clear diamond-shaped central negative space. Petals rotate subtly as if opening, but remain a very simple memorable symbol with a bold silhouette. NOT hollow capsule rings, not a butterfly, not a flower illustration, not the LaunchOS logo. Light icon: porcelain white rounded-square base, graphite satin-metal petals, subtle tasteful extruded edges. Dark icon: near-black warm graphite base, pale silver petals with restrained luminous white bevels, maintain high contrast. Very subtle native macOS dimensional material, crisp and restrained, short soft contact shadow. Flat front view, not tilted. Looks sophisticated and immediately legible at 32px. No letters, no text, no watermark, no extra UI. Balanced spacing, avoid overdecorated glossy 3D, no rainbow, no harsh bloom. This is an original icon inspired only by the simplicity, black/white contrast and compact four-unit organization common to launcher utilities.
